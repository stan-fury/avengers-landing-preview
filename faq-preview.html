Does Avengers write to our CRM or send emails for us?

No, not on its own. Avengers reads your tools. A status changes only when a person on your team sets it, and follow-ups are saved as drafts in your Gmail. Nothing is sent without you pressing send.

Do you train AI models on our data?

No. Avengers uses Claude by Anthropic through its business API, which does not train on customer data. Your emails and accounts are used only to answer your questions and prepare your day.CONFIRM vendor

How long does it take to get started?

Sign in with Google and your calendar and inbox are connected in a minute. CRM, billing and support tools take an afternoon, and we do the import with you.CONFIRM timing

We run customer success in a spreadsheet. Can we move?

Yes, that is who we built it for. Point us at the sheet, the Airtable base or the Notion table and we move your data in for free. You can keep the old sheet running while you switch.

Which tools do you connect to?

Gmail, Google Calendar, HubSpot, Chargebee, Airtable, Intercom, Jira and Slack today. If your tool is not on the list, our team builds the connector for you.CONFIRM list

Where is our data stored?

On our cloud provider in a single region, encrypted in transit. Only the people you invite can see your book.CONFIRM region and encryption at rest

What counts as a seat?

One seat is one person who signs in and owns a book of accounts. Leadership who only view the team's books do not need a paid seat.CONFIRM pricing rule

Is there a free trial?

Starter is self-serve, so you can sign up and use it with your own book. For Team and Scale we run a guided pilot on your real accounts.CONFIRM offer

What happens to our data if we leave?

You can export your accounts, statuses and notes at any time. When you close your workspace we delete your data.CONFIRM export and deletion
