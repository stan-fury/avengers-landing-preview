Security

Your customers' data, handled like our own.

Avengers reads your tools to prepare your day. Here is exactly what it can touch, what it never does, and who can see what.

What Avengers never does

✓Never sends an email for you: follow-ups wait as drafts
✓Never changes a status on its own: a person sets it
✓Never writes to your CRM without your action
✓Never trains AI models on your dataCONFIRM

Read-only by default

Avengers connects with the narrowest access each tool allows and asks for more only when you choose a feature that needs it.

GmailReads your threads with clients and can create drafts. It has no permission to send.
CalendarReads your meetings to prepare calls. It does not create, move or cancel events.CONFIRM
CRM and billingReads accounts, deals, invoices and payments. Writing back is off unless you turn it on.CONFIRM per tool

Who can see what

Access is given to named people, not to a whole email domain.

Invite-only sign-inPeople sign in with Google. Only the people you invite get in, and removing someone ends their access right away.
RolesAdmin, Leadership and Member. A CSM sees their own book; leadership sees the whole team.
Single sign-onGoogle sign-in on every plan. SAML SSO on Scale.CONFIRM SAML

AI and your data

Avengers uses Claude by Anthropic through its business API. Your data is sent only to answer the question in front of you, and it is not used to train models.CONFIRM vendor and terms

Only what is neededEach answer gets the smallest set of records it needs, not your whole book.CONFIRM
Every line has a sourceAnswers cite the email, invoice or call behind them, so a person can check before acting.
A person decidesAI suggests a status or a next step. Your team sets it.
